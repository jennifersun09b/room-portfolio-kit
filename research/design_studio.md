# Designer / illustrator studio: real-world references

Room: `templates/design_studio.py` (themes: airy, studio_night)

room you see in studio-tour articles. It has a steel-framed window, a split-top drafting table, a plan chest,
an IVAR shelf, a pegboard, a cork moodboard, a paint trolley and a wooden H-frame easel on a drop cloth.

## Room language

This is a real working studio, not a showroom. Light comes from the left through a slim steel-framed window
with a deep sill and an old column radiator under it. That is the north-light setup illustrators ask for,
and it lights a wooden H-frame easel standing on a paint-splattered cotton drop cloth. The back wall is
painted brick. On it, a cork moodboard with pinned A3 prints, swatch strips and index cards hangs over a
wooden A1 plan chest. The plan chest doubles as a second work surface, with a cutting mat, paper stacks and a
sketchbook on top. Beside it is the split-top drafting table: you draw on the tilting part, and the fixed side
holds the all-in-one computer, a pen tablet and a keyboard. A clamp-on spring-arm lamp leans over the board,
and a SKÅDIS pegboard holds tools above. A steel Tolix stool is pulled up at the board. A RÅSKOG trolley
of paints is parked by the easel. An IVAR pine shelf with books and kraft boxes stands by the door wall with
the studio name board above it. Finished canvases hang on the wall or lean with their stretchers showing.
Materials are honest: maple and pine, cork, cotton duck, powder-coated steel, glazed ceramic, painted brick
and pine floorboards. The cosy layer comes from the fiddle-leaf fig, warm lamp light, and the paint colours
of the palette spread over everything.

## Review pass (art director)

- **Palette:** the airy theme used four primaries close to a well-known tech brand's colours. They are now
  artists' pigments, cobalt blue, cadmium red, yellow ochre and viridian, muted a little like gouache. The
  night theme was shifted the same way.
- **Default artwork:** every picture used to show the same sun-and-hill clip-art. Each now shows a different
  kind of work you would find in a real illustrator's studio, all painted flat:
  - easel canvas: a landscape in progress, with layered hills, cypress trees and a ploughed field
  - hung canvas and framed print: a potted plant in front of an arch
  - moodboard prints: a Swiss-style poster, a leaf-and-dot repeat pattern, and a sheet of thumbnails
  - computer: the landscape open in an illustration app, with a swatch panel
- **Scene text:** a skill card read "Figma", which is a brand name. The cards now say Illustration, UI / UX,
  Branding and Typography.
- **Detail fixes:**
  - the radiator now has round-edged tubes and continuous hubs, as on a real column radiator
  - book spines have title blocks and head and tail bands
  - the plan chest is a darker oak, so it no longer merges with the maple table and pine floor
  - the armchair throw now drapes over the cushion instead of standing off it like a board
  - the window glass is rougher, so the moon no longer leaves a hot streak at night
  - a stray floorboard that stuck out past the front edge of the floor has been fixed
