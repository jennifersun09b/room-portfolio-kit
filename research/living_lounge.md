## Room language

This is a normal, lived-in Scandinavian flat in the style of an IKEA catalogue spread. A low, deep fabric sofa sits against the back wall with a salon-style frame wall above it. A POÄNG and a HEKTAR form a reading corner. A white BESTÅ bench with oak fronts sits under a wall-mounted TV. A striped flatweave rug with a LACK table fixes the seating area. A fiddle-leaf fig stands by the window, and a low BILLY carries books and a letter board.

Materials are honest and everyday: oak boards, plaster walls, woven cotton/linen upholstery, birch veneer, white melamine, powder-coated steel and glazed ceramic pots. The miniature look comes from the isometric camera, the soft light and the palette, not from inflating the furniture. Proportions stay at catalogue dimensions, tops and shelves are thin, and the edges have only small bevels.

## Review pass (art-director fixes)

- **Cushions are now sewn cushions, not boxes.** The scatter cushions and the three SÖDERHAMN back cushions use a stuffed-cushion shape: two fabric faces joined at a seam, fat in the middle, with the edges pulled in between the corners. Back cushions are boxier (plump foam/fibre fill), scatter cushions have the typical "ears".
- **Fabric reads as woven cotton.** Upholstery now has almost no specular gloss and half-strength colour mottling, so the sofa no longer looks like blotchy crushed velvet and the lit tops do not wash out grey.
- **POÄNG cushion** is one continuous quilted cushion with sunk stitch lines between the channels, instead of separate stepped blocks with gaps.
- **Finer, quieter grain**: the oak-veneer BESTÅ fronts, the birch POÄNG frame and the oak floor boards had oversized swirly figure (read as zebrawood/laminate); grain is now finer and lower-contrast, and the smooth-theme floor is a less orange natural oak.
- **Pouffe** rebuilt as a domed knitted drum (the old ribs stuck out over the top like candles on a cake).
- **Dog bed** bolster now has a round cross-section all the way round (it was a squashed flat ring).

## Second review pass (independent art director)

What still read as generic or "AI-made" after the first build, and what was changed:

- **The dog was a plush toy.** It was a ball body with a ball head, a white ball snout and no legs, sitting *on* a bed too small for any dog. Now a 70 cm donut bed holds a small beagle-type dog curled nose-to-tail. It has a tapered body following the curl, a folded hind leg, a long muzzle with a dark nose, floppy ears, closed eyes, front legs tucked under the chin, a tail wrapped round to the nose, and a collar with a tag.
- **POÄNG showed bare plywood at the knees.** The cushion started 12 cm behind the front of the shell and was thin. Like the real chair, it now covers the whole shell, rolls over the front lip, is 8.5 cm thick, and has six quilted channels.
- **The fiddle-leaf fig had a bamboo trunk and faceted, featureless leaves.** The trunk was a chain of short rods with visible joints. It is now one smooth tube that tapers towards the top. The leaves have twice the outline resolution, a slightly wavy margin, a pale midrib, and a glossier, darker green. The snake plant gets the yellow leaf margin of the common 'Laurentii' form.
- **The lamp arm looked like a braided cable** (joints between rod segments). The arm and the floor cable are now smooth tubes.
- **The curtains were perfect sine-wave sheets.** Each fold now has its own depth. The panels have tight gathers under the rings, deeper folds that fan out towards the hem, and a hem that just touches the floor. Ring hooks connect the curtain to the rings.
- **The pouffe looked like a jelly mould** (capsule "ribs" stuck onto a drum). The cable-knit braids are now part of the surface: they bulge round the middle, twist like a cable and flatten into the seams. Real 33 cm height.
- **The front half of the room was an empty showroom floor.** The pouffe moves to the POÄNG as its footrest. A GLADOM-style tray table stands by the chair, and a seagrass basket with rolled throws stands beside the reading corner. The dog bed sits in the open floor in front of the rug.
