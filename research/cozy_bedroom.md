## Room language

Real teen and student bedrooms are built from a handful of flat-pack classics: a MALM or HEMNES bed pushed
against the back wall, a KALLAX standing upright as the bookcase (holding boxes, books and records), and a MICKE desk
under the window with an ODGER-type chair and a TERTIAL lamp clamped to the back edge. The cosy layer is what
makes it personal. There are cotton-ball or globe fairy lights draped over the headboard, two big framed prints above the
bed, a felt letter board, a poster on a wooden hanger, a large monstera in the corner, a jute rug under
the foot of the bed, and a donut cat bed on the floor. The finishes are white paper foil (desk, KALLAX), white-stained pine
(HEMNES), oak veneer (bed), linen and knit textiles, a jute rug, an oak strip floor and painted plaster walls with white
woodwork. The palette themes recolour the textiles, walls and accents, while the construction, proportions and
materials stay true to the real products.

## Review pass (art director, kit v3)

What still looked generated after the first realism build, and what was changed:

| Gap seen in the render | Real-world reference | Fix in `templates/cozy_bedroom.py` |
|---|---|---|
| Duvet read as a thin painted slab, and the corners of the duvet and throw collapsed into spiky "dog-ear" flaps | A filled duvet (about 10.5 tog) domes 4-5 cm above the mattress. At a corner a duvet or throw falls into one soft diagonal droop | `drape()` takes `puff`, giving a flat-topped dome with rounded edges. Corners are now built as a fan, so the hem runs smoothly from the side drop to the foot drop. The cuff and throw sit on the dome |
| The throw was a flat sheet | Chunky rib-knit throw blanket | Rib columns about 3.6 cm apart, pushed out along the cloth's normal so they carry on down the hanging sides |
| Walls, curtains and bedding had cloudy, blotchy mottling that looked like stains | Matt emulsion paint and plain linen/cotton look calm | `smat()` now softens the pattern by default: plaster 0.35, fabric 0.45, knit 0.55 |
| The jute rug was a plain beige rectangle | IKEA LOHALS flatwoven jute: rows of twisted yarn with a darker yarn now and then, and bound edges | About 160 woven 1.4 cm ribs in three natural tones, plus the binding |
| The nightstand top showed rippled "water ring" grain | Solid pine: straight grain along tops, drawer fronts and rails | A second pine material with the grain along X, used on the horizontal parts |
| Books looked like candy blocks | Real spines: muted shades, a mix of white and dark, printed title bands | Each book gets one of 3 shade steps, and 70% get one or two title bands |
| The lamp cord ended in mid-air, and the fairy-light cable ran to nothing | Cords run over the back of the nightstand to a wall socket | The cord drops down the back. A single socket with a plug sits beside the nightstand |
| The cat was a plain orange blob, and the donut bed looked like an inflatable ring | A ginger tabby on a shaggy faux-fur "calming donut" | Soft broken tabby stripes (wave texture into KIT_BASE). A cloud-displacement shag on the bolster and cushion |

## Review pass 2 (independent art director)

A fresh-eyes review rendered close-up crops of every piece. These were the gaps that still looked generated or broken:

| Gap seen in the render | Real-world reference | Fix in `templates/cozy_bedroom.py` |
|---|---|---|
| The chair seat was a square slab.
