## Room language (Oxford college room / Victorian study)
Dark-panelled lower walls (frame-and-panel dado, tall skirting) under deep green painted plaster; a picture rail
and a cornice at the ceiling. One wall is entirely built-in bookcases: deeper cupboards at the bottom, open
shelving above, a cornice on top and a brass rail carrying a hook-on library ladder - with a plaster bust on one shelf,
a nod to the busts on plinths along Trinity's Long Room. The fireplace is the focal point: a pale marble
chimneypiece with an arched cast-iron insert, tiled slips, a coal grate, a brass fender and fire irons, a bracket clock,
candlesticks and a ginger jar on the shelf, and an oil painting above it lit by a brass picture light. A partners' desk sits under a
tall sash window with heavy velvet curtains, lit by a green banker's lamp; a deep-buttoned oxblood Chesterfield
wing chair with its matching footstool, a tripod wine table with tea, a brass standard lamp and a Heriz rug on oak herringbone parquet make the
reading corner. Materials: mahogany/walnut, oxblood leather, brass, marble, cast iron, wool pile, velvet.

## Realism review pass (second art-director pass)
What still read as "AI-made" after the first rebuild, compared against the real references above, and how it was fixed:

| Gap seen in the render | Real-world reference look | Fix in the template |
|---|---|---|
| Rug field was a regular grid of scattered squares (pixel-art look) | Heriz carpets: large serrated geometric medallion on its own ground, end pendants, ivory corner spandrels, angular vine trellis across a brick-red field, rosette-and-vine main border, reciprocal guard stripes, abrash (dye-lot) colour bands | The whole design is painted from the theme colours on a 2 cm "knot block" grid (`heriz_design`) and baked into the wool material, so outlines step like hand knotting; abrash bands + per-knot colour variation |
| Globe showed noise blobs (fantasy map) | 18in library globes: real coastlines, outline-coloured countries in pale washes, brown coastlines with coastal shading, graticule, equator + ecliptic, amber varnish | Simplified real coastlines (lon/lat polygons) rasterised to an equirectangular map with pastel country washes, coastal shading, 15-degree graticule, equator and ecliptic, patchy varnish; mapped on a lon/lat UV sphere |
| Chesterfield "buttoning" was a net of dark rods on a flat panel | Hand-tufted Chesterfield wingbacks: puffed diamonds with creases between the buttons, brass nailhead trim round the scroll arms and along the seat rail, piped cushion | `tufted()` quilted leather sheet (puff between buttons, valleys along the diamond edges) on the back, inner wings and footstool top; brass nailhead rows; cushion piping |
| Fire: cone flames and white balls | Victorian register grate: heaped faceted coal, a glowing ember bed, licking orange flames with yellow cores, an ash-pan fret | 60 faceted coal lumps heaped in the basket (inner ones glow), wavy swept flame tongues with emission-only orange/yellow, grate bed and slotted fret; crisp carved roundels on the corbels |

## Third pass (independent art-director review)
