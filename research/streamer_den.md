## Room language

A real streamer / gamer bedroom-studio, the kind seen in set-up tours: a dark painted wall (navy at night,
off-white by day) above a satin skirting board, an oak-laminate floor in 19 cm planks with staggered joints,
a uPVC casement window with a roller blind, and LED strip tape stuck along the top of the walls. The
battlestation sits in the corner on an L-desk against a wall of acoustic foam wedges, with the neon name
sign (LED neon flex on a tinted acrylic board with metal stand-offs) above. The "couch gaming" half of the
room is a KALLAX laid on its side as a TV bench under a wall-mounted 65" TV, a KLIPPAN loveseat under the
window with a LACK side table, and an Arcade1Up-style cabinet in the corner under a cluster of hexagon light
panels. Light comes from the monitors, the RGB tower, the LED strips and the ring light (night), or a big
soft window (day).

## Review pass (art-director fixes)

- The racing chair now stands pulled up to the desk, facing the screens, like a real set-up, rather than
  swivelled out into the middle of the room. Its backrest has padded side wings that wrap forward, plus a
  stitched rear cover.
- The sofa throw used to be a hard rectangular slab. It is now a folded chunky-knit throw laid over the arm.
- The rug is now a striped flat-weave with a fringe (it was a plain slab with a glowing stripe).
- The oak grain is finer (scale 3.6), and the PC fans have pitched blades.
- By day the LED strips no longer paint a hot band on the walls. The seat fill lights were moved away from the walls.
- A knitted pouffe fills the empty floor in front of the sofa, and the snake plant moved from the open front
  corner to the back wall.

## Review pass 2 (independent art-director fixes)

- **Monitor V-foot:** it was drawn the wrong way round, with the point at the front, so it looked like two sticks lying
  on the desk. It is now a real V: a hub under the column at the back, two arms reaching forward and out under the
  screen, and rubber pads at the tips. It now stands on the desk mat instead of clipping through it.
- **Racing chair:** the outline is now a real racing seat. Wide shoulder wings at about 60 % height step in
  through a clear notch to a narrower head section with a rounded top. The harness slots sit at the notch. A
  contrast stitch line runs round the rear cover, the side you see from the camera.
- **Glass PC case:** the panes are now visibly tinted glass. Real cases have a black silk-screened border along the
  top, bottom and outer edges, and that is now there too, with no pillar where the panes meet. Through the glass
  you see VRM heatsinks, the chipset cover, a sleeved 24-pin cable and a GPU backplate. The top vents are finer,
  and the fan blades now sit inside their RGB rings.
- **Painted surfaces:** walls, the roller blind, the sofa, the cushions, the rug and the KALLAX boxes had cloudy
  blotches that read as stains or stucco. surface() now runs at a finer scale on these, so they read as even
  emulsion paint and woven fabric. Laminate planks now vary by about +/-6 % instead of about +/-12 %.
- **Arcade cabinet:** the marquee said "ARCADE", which no real cabinet does. It now carries pixel-art key art
  instead (a ship and two speed bands), with no text. The huge amber coin entries looked like a pair of eyes;
  they are now small, lit red coin inserts with slots.
- **Knitted pouffe:** this was a drum covered in separate balls. It is now 26 columns of real "V" knit stitches,
  each made of two leaning loops, built as one mesh.
- **Snake plant:** it is now a Sansevieria laurentii. The lance-shaped leaves are narrow at the base and widest
  in the middle, with pale zig-zag cross-bands and the yellow leaf margin. The glazed pot now has a rim.
