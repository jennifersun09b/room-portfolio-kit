# Lantern Alley Ramen Bar: real-world references

Room id: `lantern_ramen_bar`. This is a themed room (kit v4): a narrow late-night ramen counter shop on a rain-wet
alley corner. It evokes the *feeling* of Japanese neighbourhood ramen counters and narrow lantern-lit drinking
alleys, plus a "cyber noodle bar" theme. All designs are original (see "IP-safety" below).

Every piece below is modelled on a real object at its real size. Product names appear only here and in the
template's `REFERENCES` comment, never in the scene.

## Notes on scale
- Counter 90 cm with a 108 cm serving ledge, stools 68 cm, lanterns hang 1.0 m above the counter, door 2.0 m,
  vending machine 1.61 m. These match SPEC section 7 (counter 90-92 cm, bar stool seat 65-75 cm, door 2.0-2.1 m).
- The alley is one step (4 cm) below the shop floor.

## IP-safety (what was deliberately avoided)
The research brief linked this room to Japanese late-night ramen counters and alley bars in general, and to the
"cyberpunk noodle bar" mood. Nothing in the template, scene, object names, comments or file names refers to any
anime, game, film or restaurant chain.

These things are deliberately **not** in the room:
- No anime ramen-stand look: no open-air stall with a short split curtain over the stools, no characters, no
  headbands, no swirl symbols, no posters of characters.
- **No pink-swirl fish-cake slice** in the bowls. It is a real topping, but it is too closely tied to one famous
  anime, so the bowls have pork, egg, bamboo shoots, nori and scallions only.
- No real chain branding: no single-seat booths with dividers, no chain logos, colours or uniforms.
- The beckoning cat is a generic ceramic figurine (raised paw, collar, bell, plain gold coin, closed smiling
  eyes). It has no bow, no character face and no text on the coin.
- No Japanese characters anywhere. The only words in the scene are generic English: "RAMEN" on the street sign,
  "TICKETS" on the machine, "No. 1-3" on the menu planks, and the user's own hooks. The neon theme uses a
  pictogram (bowl, chopsticks, steam) instead of any lettering.
- Neon theme: "cyberpunk" is used only as a generic mood. No game or film logos, no famous film sign, no
  dragon signs, no city skyline.
- The poster default is a simple sun, hill and sea graphic, not a copy of any well-known print.
- No real brand names on bottles, the ticket machine, the TV or the bicycle. Bottle labels are plain paper with
  one dark mark.
