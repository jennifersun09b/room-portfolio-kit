# Tiny Planet Stargazer: real-world references

Room: `templates/tiny_planet_stargazer.py` (themes `sunset_hour`, `starry_night`). This is a themed room (kit v4). It is a small round planet floating in a dusk sky, with a garden observatory, a stargazing deck and a few garden curiosities. The room captures a general feeling (a lonely, poetic little world under the stars) using original designs. Product and maker names appear only in this file and in code comments. The scene has no logos, brand names or text apart from the portfolio hooks.

## Diorama base

The two-wall box is replaced by a floating planet, as SPEC section 8 allows. A rocky ball (radius 2.55 m) has a gently domed lawn on top, and craters, pebbles and small crystal outcrops on its underside. Everything stands on the lawn through `ground(x, y)`, and level stone pads are used where a piece needs a flat footing. All 9 slots stay inside the 5.2 x 5.2 m square, and the script sets `kit_room_size = [5.2, 5.2, 3.5]`. `kit_bounds` is larger, so it covers the floating moons and stepping stones.

## Portfolio hooks
- **Name and role:** on the painted sign board over the observatory door, in cream and gold letters. The name is 0.125 m tall and the role 0.09 m.
- **Pictures:** a star-chart poster (0.78 x 0.5 m) and two framed pictures (0.32 x 0.42 m) on the observatory's side wall, facing the camera, each with a brass title plate (46 or 34 cm wide, 4.2 cm letters).
- **Links:** the paper plane is `email`. The three floating mini-moons are `github`, `linkedin` and `website`, and one of them has a ring.
- **Tagline:** on a long painted name board (2.4 x 0.24 m, like a ship's quarterboard) fixed on iron brackets to the front of the rock, in cream letters up to 11 cm tall, so even a full sentence stays readable.

## IP-safety

The room only evokes a general genre: a tiny lonely planet under the stars, with a garden observatory. Several classic stories and games share that image, and the room must not lean on any single one of them. This is what we deliberately avoided:
- **Names:** no title, character name, place or asteroid designation, and no author name, anywhere in the scene, object names, comments or file names. The file id is the neutral `tiny_planet_stargazer` and the themes are `sunset_hour` and `starry_night`.
- **Characters and lookalikes:** no figures of any kind, no animals, and no costume pieces. The only textile on the chair is a wide knitted throw in lilac and cream.
- **No signature prop stack:** a tiny planet plus "a rose under a glass dome", little cone-shaped volcanoes, a watering can by the flower, spiky trees, animal companions or an aviator's plane would together restage one famous book. The earlier builds had several of these. We removed all of them:
  - The two cone "volcano" lamps became an **amethyst geode lamp** and a **quartz crystal cluster** (real decor products).
  - The **rose under a glass cloche** became a **brass display orrery** with no glass dome. Our own fairytale room also treats the rose under a bell jar as franchise-only, so we do not use it on a tiny planet either.
  - The **biplane desk model** became a **planisphere and a red-light torch**, which is real stargazing kit.
  - The striped **scarf** on the chair became a **knitted throw**.
  - The **crescent-moon swing** on the lamp post became a **hanging flower basket** (it also read as a weighing-scale pan).
  - There are no spiky or bottle-shaped trees: the garden uses clipped round shrubs, lavender, asters and daisies. The watering can stands by the lavender trough.
- **Generic props only:** the observatory, brass refractor, deck chair, teak table, orrery, geode lamp, lamp post with a lantern and basket, steamer trunk, star globe, moons and paper plane are everyday or astronomy objects that exist in shops, gardens and many unrelated works.
- **Style and text:** no watercolour storybook illustration style, quotes, maps or dedications. The only text in the scene is the portfolio placeholders ("YOUR NAME", "your role", "PROJECT ONE..." and "your tagline here").
- **No logos or brands** on any product: the brass telescope, deck chair, teak table, geode lamp and lamp post carry no maker marks.
- **Marketing:** describe the room as "tiny planet", "cosy stargazer" or "little observatory planet". Do not mention any book, film or game in store titles, tags or descriptions.

## Art-direction review (render passes)
- Pass 1 (IP and readability): orrery instead of the rose under glass, planisphere and torch instead of the biplane, a throw instead of the scarf. The quartz cluster used to merge into a white blob that read like a little figurine, so it was rebuilt as seven clearly different points with a pale core. The brass disc tray on the telescope tripod read as a floating yellow disc, so it is now a triangular teak tray with two eyepieces.
- Pass 2 (hooks): the tagline plaque on the lamp post was far too small to read at 1920x1080, and a real one-sentence tagline from profile.json shrank to a few pixels. The tagline now sits on a 2.4 m name board on the front of the rock, which also fills the empty rock face. The role text went from 7.4 to 9 cm, and the title plates and letters are bigger.
- Pass 3 (silhouettes): the crescent swing and its two ropes read as a weighing-scale pan, so the arm now carries a hanging flower basket. The paper plane was seen from behind as a flat triangle that looked like a UI arrowhead. The new flight path turns it side-on, and it has a dihedral, a keel and creases. The orrery was too small under the glass, so the dome was removed and the orrery is shown at 1.4x on a walnut plinth.
