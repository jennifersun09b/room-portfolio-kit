# Wizard School Tower Dorm: real-world references

Room: `templates/wizard_school_dorm.py` (themes `candlelit_night`, `autumn_term`). The room is the top room of a round stone tower in an invented boarding academy. It is a shared bedroom with two four-poster beds, travelling trunks, a fireplace on a chimney breast with a cauldron by the hearth, a writing desk with an orrery, a bookcase of old leather books, a barn owl in a brass domed cage by the window, black school robes on a coat stand, a besom on the wall, a painted starry vault with candles floating under it, and a spiral stair that comes up through the floor. It is built to evoke the general "magic school" or "dark academia boarding school" feeling only, pushed as far as the genre goes while every design stays original.

Every piece is a real object you could buy at an antiques dealer, a bed shop or a bird-of-prey centre, or see in a real castle. These are Victorian and Edwardian four-posters and steamer trunks, a Tudor-arch stone fire surround, a Queen Anne wing chair, an English Windsor chair, a brass desk orrery, a cast cauldron, a birch besom and a barn owl. The tower itself follows Scottish and English medieval tower houses: coursed ashlar walls about 0.3 m thick, splayed lancet windows, a newel stair and battlements. Brand and dealer names appear only in this file and in code comments. The scene has no logos or trademarked graphics.

## Room language

- **Round tower room**: a single round chamber about 5.6 m across. The walls are coursed ashlar in grey-blue sandstone, with courses 0.22 to 0.32 m high laid in pale lime mortar. Dressed (smoother, lighter) stone is used for the window surrounds, the coping and the fire surround. The parapet is crenellated, with merlons about 0.56 m wide and crenels about 0.4 m. A chimney stack with two clay pots rises above it.
- **Painted vault and floating candles**: the top 52 cm of the wall (two courses under the coping) is plastered and painted as a night sky in deep blue, with about 30 gilt stars per metre, small constellations joined by fine gold lines and two gilt crescents, above a gilt bead; it steps up over the window surrounds and carries on across the top of the chimney breast. About two dozen church candles (11-20 cm) hang in the air between 2.3 and 2.9 m. Their positions are chosen after the camera is set, and any candle that would cover a portfolio hook, a link object, the lancet, the cage or the fire is dropped, so they never hide your content.
- **Windows**: a tall single-light lancet with an equilateral pointed arch over the desk, and a round oculus high in the wall above the head of the first bed. Through the lancet you see a loch with moonlight glinting on it, a dark pine forest on the far shore, low hills and a range of jagged snow-capped peaks under a starry sky (autumn: turning trees and a golden sky). Both have a splayed inner reveal (wide inside, narrow at the glass), as in castle walls, cut through a solid dressed-stone surround. The lancet has a stone window board inside, leaded diamond quarry glazing and two iron saddle bars; the oculus is a small wheel window (eight lead spokes between a hub ring and an outer ring).
- **Shared dorm furniture**: two single four-posters with open testers (frame and pleated pelmet, no roof cloth, so the beds read from above), each with a steamer trunk at the foot. The first bed stands square along the left curve of the wall with its curtains drawn along the wall side and tied back on the room side. The second bed stands out on the open (cut-away) side of the room, angled so that its tester rails sit low in the picture and never cross the writing desk; all four of its curtains are tied back to the posts with gold tassels and it carries pelmets only at the head and foot. The hearth and a fireside wing chair sit between the beds, with the bookcase beside the chimney breast, the writing desk and Windsor chair under the lancet, the owl cage on its stand beside the desk and the coat stand of robes against the right-hand wall.
- **Floor**: wide oak boards (180 mm) in random lengths, with a ring of dressed kerb stones round the edge. A spiral stair comes up through a stone-kerbed well at the front of the room, beside the first trunk; you step down on the side facing the room, between two short oak handrails on turned balusters, with an iron lantern on the kerb.

## Notes for modelling
- Bed: mattress top 55 cm, post blocks 75 mm square, turned posts 50-80 mm across, footboard rail 62 cm, tester at 2.15 m.
- Thin parts stay thin: desk top 22 mm, shelves 18 mm, chair spindles 13 mm, trunk slats 12 mm, lead cames 6 mm.
- Bevels are 2-8 mm on wood and metal, 10-12 mm on stone. Soft bevels (20-50 mm) are used only on cushions, quilts and pillows.
- Light comes from the fire, candles (mantel candlesticks, a desk chamberstick, the bookcase, the lancet window board, a lantern by the stair, and every third floating candle), the moon or a low sun in the windows, a soft glow from the stair well below and a faint green-white glow over the cauldron. The "magic" is limited to genre cues: candles floating (lit by day too), the painted sky, the brew's glow and a few motes round the orrery at night.
- New props stay clear of the walls: the broom hooks stand 20 cm off the curved wall so the straight besom clears the stones along its length, the cage stand's feet and the second trunk stay inside the floor kerb.
- Slots are axis-aligned boxes in the kit checker, so the big pieces (bed, trunk, desk, bookcase) stand square to the room along the curved wall; the second bed and trunk are decoration and can follow the curve.

## IP safety (what was deliberately avoided)

The room only evokes a genre: a castle-tower bedroom at a magic school, alongside "dark academia", "cottagecore castle" and real medieval tower-house interiors. These are the rules we followed (described generically on purpose, so this file does not itself list any franchise's designs):

- **No franchise, school, house, character, place, spell or creature names** anywhere: scene, object names, comments, file names, theme names or tags. The room id is the generic `wizard_school_dorm` and the themes are `candlelit_night` and `autumn_term`.
- **No crests, emblems or heraldry.** Nothing is carved or painted over the banner except the generic painted night sky (random stars, constellation lines, crescents). The only carvings are the plain architectural roundels in the fire surround's spandrels. The banner carries only the user's own name and role.
- **No colour scheme borrowed from a story.** Textiles are plum velvet with teal and gold thread trim. The scarf is deliberately three colours (oatmeal, teal, rust) so it cannot read as a two-colour school scarf at thumbnail size; an earlier plum-and-mustard version was dropped because at small sizes it drifted towards a well-known dark-red-and-gold pairing. The autumn theme keeps its rust and mustard in the trees, squashes and leaves outside the textiles.
- **No heraldic or story animals.** The pet is an ordinary buff-gold barn owl, a common real species, in a plain brass cage, carrying nothing. The email link is an ordinary letter rack on the desk.
- **No signature costume designs.** The robes are plain black academic gowns with hoods: no badges, crests, coloured linings or trims, ties or hats. The scarf is an oatmeal knit with teal and rust stripes, which does not match any well-known school-house colour set.
- **No signature props.** The broom is a plain garden besom (straight hazel handle, bound birch twigs), not a sports or racing broom. The wand is a plain carved stick in a pen box, with no specific handle design, and it is not named or labelled. The floating candles and the painted starry vault are generic enchanted-castle imagery with original, randomly generated star patterns. Nothing in the room belongs to one particular story: no sports equipment, signature accessories or symbols, scoring systems, maps, tickets or named products, no franchise typefaces, spell names or quotes.
- **No lookalikes.** The portraits are faceless painterly studies of nobody in particular; the default paintings are a landscape, a still life and a moon over a hill. No figure resembles a character or an actor.
- **Generic genre props only**: four-poster beds, steamer trunks, a fireplace, a cauldron, an orrery, a telescope, a quill and sealing wax, scrolls, leather books, chess, pewter goblets, a besom, an owl in a cage, robes on a coat stand, floating candles, a painted sky, a spiral stair and battlements. These all exist in real castles, antiques shops or many unrelated fantasy works.
- **No brand marks** on the products the pieces are based on. Dealer and product names appear only in code comments and in this file.
- **Marketing**: describe the room as "wizard school", "magic academy" or "castle tower dorm". Do not name or tag any franchise in store titles, tags or descriptions.

## Art-direction review (independent pass)

- The carved roundel over the banner read as a small green face at portfolio size. Replaced it with an architectural quatrefoil boss and a gilt star (also removes the "school crest" idea entirely); the closer-vibe pass later removed the boss in favour of the painted night sky.
- The second bed's tester, pelmet and head curtains hid the writing desk, so the orrery (website link) and letter rack (email link) were almost invisible. The second bed now has its pelmet only on the wall side and foot and no head curtains; the desk, orrery and letters read clearly.
- The stair well read as a water well (one tread, a pale stump). The treads now wind down from an opening facing the room with oak nosings, an oak newel post rises from the stone newel, and short handrails flank the opening.
- Fire flames rendered as white cones; they are now orange outer tongues with yellow cores.
- The oculus faced away from the camera and its lead wheel looked like cross-hairs; it moved round to face the camera more and is now an eight-spoke wheel with hub and rim rings. The moon glints on the loch replace a single bright bar, and the sky moon has a smooth glow instead of four stepped rings.
- The quill read as a white cable; it now has a broad feather vane. The role line on the banner is larger. Autumn term gains squashes on the hearth, a bowl of apples on the trunk and larger fallen leaves.

## Closer-vibe pass (genre push)

Goal: make the room read as "magic boarding-school tower dorm at night" at a glance, as strongly as the genre allows, while staying original.

- **Added**: the painted starry vault band and about two dozen floating candles; a brass domed owl cage on a stand by the window (the barn owl moved into it, turned to face you); a besom on two iron wall hooks; plain black hooded robes on a bentwood coat stand; a striped knitted scarf on the second bed; a cauldron on a trivet by the hearth (replacing the copper kettle); a brass telescope and sealed letters on the window board; a chess game and sealed scrolls on the second trunk; an open wand box and a brass name plate (second `name` hook) on the first trunk; a third spell book on the desk stack; two pewter goblets on the mantel; two gilt portraits (frame hooks 4 and 5) with title plaques (`title` hooks 4 and 5); mountains with snow caps and a pine forest in the lancet view.
- **Rearranged**: the second bed moved out onto the open side of the room with every curtain tied back, so its tester rails no longer cross the writing desk; the spiral stair moved to the front beside the first trunk; the oculus dropped slightly so the painted band can run above it; the lancet is a little wider and taller, with larger quarries so the view reads.
- **Render critique rounds**: (1) the first vault was a deep plaster cove under the coping, but from the camera only its pale top ledge showed, so it became a painted band on the wall face; floating candles read as white sticks, so they gained larger flames and soft halos; the closed curtains of the second bed were removed. (2) Candles bunched over the first bed, so they are spread across the picture (no more than three in any vertical slice); the broom's head was buried in the curved wall, so the hooks now stand 20 cm off the wall and the broom is shorter; the mountain range was the same blue as the sky, so the peaks became dark silhouettes with bright snow caps. (3) The wand box was dark on dark, so it gained a pale satin lining; the cage stand's feet reached the kerb, so the slot moved 10 cm into the room; the cauldron steam read as balloons, so it became a single faint wisp.


## Independent IP and art-direction review (fresh eyes)

- **IP**: object names, text bodies, comments and this file were checked for franchise, school, house, character, place and spell names; none were found. The one risk was colour: the plum-and-mustard scarf read as a dark-red-and-gold house scarf at portfolio size, so it is now an oatmeal knit with teal and rust stripes. The research notes describing a carved boss over the banner were out of date (the boss was removed) and now describe the room as built.
- **Floating candles**: several candles were drawn right on top of the bed testers, so they looked as if they stood on the beds. Candidates whose candle would touch a tester rail in the picture are now dropped, as are any that would cover the besom.
- **Besom**: its handle was hidden behind the first bed's tester and only the twig head showed. It now hangs 11 cm higher on hooks at 186 and 196 degrees, with the head under the round window, so the whole broom reads above the canopy.
- **Lancet view**: the loch was hard to read as water. A full moon with a soft halo now sits low over the peaks, and a wider path of glints runs down the loch beneath it.
- **Spiral stair**: the treads were in shadow, so the well read as a water well. A stronger uplight and a small light on the far treads now pick out the winding steps.
- **Owl and banner**: the owl is shown at 96 % (was 86 %) so its face reads through the cage wires, and the role line on the banner is larger and brighter (11 cm, was 9 cm).
