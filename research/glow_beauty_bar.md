# Glow Nail & Beauty Bar: real-world references

Room: `templates/glow_beauty_bar.py` (themes `milk_blush`, `jelly_neon`). This is a small boutique nail, lash and brow bar of the kind independent nail techs and beauty artists run and photograph for social media. It is an original room: it only evokes the "social-media salon" feeling (pink terrazzo, arch niches, a neon script sign on a hedge wall, a colour-sorted polish wall). Product names appear only in this file and in code comments. The scene has no logos, brand names, real packaging or trademarked graphics.

## Venue and room language

A boutique beauty bar is usually one small room behind a shopfront. Guests are welcomed at a reception desk with a booking tablet and a card reader. The walls are milky white or blush plaster with painted arch niches and thin brass trims. A wall of nail polish, sorted by colour on clear acrylic ledges, sits behind the manicure station. The manicure table has a white top, a dust-collector vent, a towel and a hand rest, an LED gel lamp, and a gooseneck task lamp. The technician sits on a gas-lift stool, the client in a velvet tub chair. The pedicure "throne" is a velvet wing chair on a raised plinth with a foot basin on a step in front of it, often set against a faux boxwood hedge with a pink LED-neon name sign. A make-up corner has an arched bulb mirror over a slim vanity and a ring light (also used to film nail art). A velvet loveseat, a round rug and a small marble table make the waiting area. Pampas grass and trailing pothos soften the room. The floor is poured or tiled terrazzo.

## IP-safety

What was deliberately avoided:
- No real polish or cosmetics brands: no brand names, logos or labels; bottle shapes are a generic square stock bottle with a plain round cap (no signature bottle or cap silhouettes of any polish or fashion house, no brand-pink packaging).
- No real salon chains, celebrity salons or booking-app logos on the tablet (the default screen is an abstract calendar grid).
- No branded hair dryers or styling tools; no recognisable ring-light or lamp branding.
- Magazines on the table are plain colour blocks with no titles or covers.
- Lettering in the scene is generic only: the neon placeholders ("Your Name", "your role"), "colour bar" over the polish wall and "services" on the default poster.
- The look is a genre feeling (boutique pastel beauty bar, the "jelly" nail trend for the evening theme), not any specific venue, franchise or character.
